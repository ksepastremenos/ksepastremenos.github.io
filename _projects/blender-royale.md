  text_en: '"Blender Royale" is a community event over at the erindale.xyz discord server. The premise is that all participants get 45 minutes to complete a render while using no pre-built assets. Then a strawpoll is created to pick the best render and fuel some friendly competition. From time to time, the rules are spiced up and there can be elimination rounds, team battles, etc.'
